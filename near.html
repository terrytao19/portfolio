NEAR - UWB Tracker

NEAR anchors

NEAR is a system to trilaterate a tag using time-of-flight UWB chirps and a central LoRa base station. These are the five assembled anchor boards. On each board, the DW1000 UWB module is on the right edge and the Seeed Studio LoRa module is at the top.

I was responsible for the PCB design and bringup for the anchor boards and the tag boards, and designed a radio protocol to minimize chirp overlaps and maximize ranging rate.
