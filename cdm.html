MRacing - CDM

CDM overview

I designed a modular PCB for quick swapping of the ECU, debug probe, GMSL2 deserializer, and GNSS receiver using M.2 connectors.

Boards: RCDM, DAP, APU-CAN, ECU, USB-HUB, DESERIALIZER, BMP, and APU-SENSE
CDM baseboard with all of the M.2 modules inserted
CDM baseboard with the SMARC module (NXP i.MX 95) inserted
Bare PCBs for the ECU, USB to CAN, and USB hub modules, fresh from the factory

The USB hub is a two input, two output hub with a high-speed and SuperSpeed mux to select the input, either through the vehicle harness or through the soldered USB connector when the board is out of the car.
